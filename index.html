GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
the first call.

Some practical things teams typically want to understand.

Do we need to replace our existing cameras?+

Not necessarily. Camex describes support for compatible existing IP camera infrastructure. Camera models, stream access and deployment requirements should be checked before implementation.

What kinds of events can AI detect?+

Capabilities depend on the configured models and deployment. The current product materials describe use cases including people counting, queue monitoring, zone alerts and selected safety analytics.

Can Camex connect to our existing systems?+

The developer materials describe API and integration options. The right approach depends on your systems, event requirements and technical environment.

How is camera footage handled?+

Data handling, retention, access controls and deployment architecture should be confirmed with the Camex team for your specific setup. Avoid assuming a particular privacy configuration without technical confirmation.
